Refund and dispute policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

All PlakhinLabs software is free. The only payments I accept are voluntary donations, and they give no access to any product or service.

If you donated by mistake or were charged more than you intended, email plakhinlabs@gmail.com within 30 days with the date and amount of the payment. I will refund it in full or help resolve the issue.
